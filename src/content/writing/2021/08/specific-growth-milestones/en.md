"I want to grow faster" easily ends up vague. I prepared this set of questions because of a real one-on-one — after 20 to 30 hours of conversation, I compiled the scattered questions into this checklist. When you talk, keep one thing in mind: growth is an accumulation of milestones, and a milestone is made up of four parts: **situation, behavior, evidence, and the next round of practice**.

## Prepare Your Own Feelings

Don't define what "growth" is yet — instead answer these concrete questions:

- How tight or loose is your work lately? Do you feel physically tired — is the schedule too tight, or are there too many things going on?
- Are you mentally tired? Do you approve of the product's current direction, or are you not quite sure?
- How does the team's current atmosphere feel: too much involution, or too busy?
  - "Involution" means grabbing work from each other over meaningless things; "busy" means there is too much work, too many schedules, and you need overtime to finish it.
- Do you have expectations about the split between business and technology? (60/40, 70/30, 50/50, ...)
- Is communication within the team still smooth? What about within your group, and across groups?

## Points to Discuss

Start from "how growth has felt recently," not from "have you hit your growth targets."

- Do you have a fairly clear sense of the nodes in your own growth?
  - Can I tell which things, done to what degree, will give me a sense of achievement and growth?
  - Or rather: do I know that each cycle brings growth, and do I accept that pace of growth?
  - What should the growth milestones in your mind look like?
- Does the direction of your work bring a sense of growth? Does cross-platform work feel tedious, like filling potholes and getting annoyed? Does full-stack work bring too much pressure and scatter your energy?
- What kind of growth do I want for myself? Am I making progress in what I am doing now? Is there a gap from my expectations?
- Does the team feel like it is growing? Compared with your own growth, does that create pressure? Can the things you do bring you growth?
- Do you feel recognized in the team? Needed? Is there something you want to push everyone to do? Do you wish more people on the team would help you?
- If I have points I want to discuss, let me set the title: my thought is ______.

Use situations instead of labels: don't ask "Am I mature enough?" but ask "Facing a task with incomplete information, can I first spell out what's known, what's unknown, and the path to verify?" Don't ask "Do I have leadership?" but ask "When there's a gap in the division of labor, can I foster collaboration with clear boundaries, rather than quietly taking on all the work?"

## Venting and Feedback

- I hope you pay a bit more attention to me.
- I feel the details of my growth plan aren't enough yet, and there are too many trivial things; I hope it can be cleaner and clearer.
- I'm being pushed too hard and can't keep up.
- I've taken on too much; I can't finish it and I'm about to explode.
- and also ______

Feedback requests can also be very specific: "In my last write-up, which part helped you make a decision? Which part made you stop and ask a follow-up?"
